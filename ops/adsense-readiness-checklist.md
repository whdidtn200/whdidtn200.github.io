# MALT Tech Blog AdSense Readiness Checklist

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## 1. Public trust pages

- [x] About page exists
- [x] Contact page exists
- [x] Privacy policy exists
- [x] Editorial standards / AI disclosure page exists
- [ ] Contact route should remain monitored and visible

## 2. Search and indexing

- [x] `robots.txt` exists
- [x] `sitemap.xml` exists
- [x] Google site verification file exists
- [x] `ads.txt` exists
- [ ] Google Search Console performance should be checked weekly

## 3. Content quality before applying

- [ ] Prepare at least 20 to 30 posts that are worth reading without ads
- [ ] Increase ratio of deep-dive / problem-solving posts versus short summaries
- [ ] For each auto-published paper post, keep these sections:
  - why this matters now
  - who should read this
  - operational checklist
  - limitations
- [ ] Reduce pages that look like thin summaries or duplicate archive material

## 4. Monetization safety checks

- [ ] Keep AI involvement openly disclosed
- [ ] Avoid fake author personas or hidden automation claims
- [ ] Avoid excessive ad density on first screen
- [ ] Keep navigation, archive, and policy pages easy to find

## 5. Recommended next build steps

1. Create 5 to 10 pillar posts targeting high-intent railway PHM / industrial AI keywords.
2. Add lightweight analytics and Search Console review rhythm.
3. Build internal links from daily arXiv posts to evergreen deep dives.
4. Apply to AdSense only after thin pages are cleaned up.
