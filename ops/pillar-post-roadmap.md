Updated: 2026-06-17

## Why this exists

Daily arXiv posts help freshness, but AdSense approval and Google search growth are more likely to come from pages that solve a stable reader problem. These pillar posts should become the main landing pages that attract search traffic and then route readers into the archive.

## On-page rules for every pillar post

- Introduce one concrete reader problem in the first screen.
- Use plain language headings instead of academic taxonomy.
- Add a comparison table, checklist, and recommended next step.
- Link out to 3 to 8 relevant existing posts.
- Keep at least one section on limitations and deployment risk.
- End with a small “where to go next” internal-link block.

## Search and monetization notes

- Target long-lived intents, not only paper titles.
- Keep AI involvement transparent.
- Prefer pages that can rank for `guide`, `how to`, `checklist`, `comparison`, and `roadmap` queries.
- Do not apply for AdSense until these pillar pages look stronger than the daily summary pages.
